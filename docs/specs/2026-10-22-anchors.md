# Anchors: the OpenAlex Frame

**Status:** design approved in conversation (decisions 79–80). **Replaces** intro-sources §5a and decision 74.

## 1. What changed, and why

Decision 74 planned to link Knowledge concepts to OpenAlex topics and ESCO skills, and to borrow their names, definitions and examples. A look at the real data changed that.

- **OpenAlex topics are clusters of research papers**, not things a person knows. "Dynamic programming" finds "Adaptive Dynamic Programming Control"; "algorithms" finds "Metaheuristic Optimization Algorithms Research". As names, definitions or "Includes" for a person's cards they would be wrong. There is nothing for Docker or poker.
- **OpenAlex's upper layers are clean**: 4 domains, 26 fields, 252 subfields ("Computer Science → Artificial Intelligence"), stable and CC0.
- **ESCO is a list of job skills.** "Dynamic programming" finds "thermodynamics", "React" finds "react calmly in stressful situations"; Docker and poker find nothing. Its bulk download sits behind a form.

So the anchors are a **frame**: the fields and subfields that say where a concept lives. Nothing about a concept's own name or content comes from them.

## 2. The frame (decision 79)

- **Data.** `packages/knowledge/data/openalex-frame.json` ships with the code: the 26 fields and 252 subfields, and for each subfield the words its research is known by (every topic's name, then its 30 strongest keywords). It is rebuilt by hand with `scripts/build-openalex-frame.mjs`. No lookup ever leaves the machine. ESCO is not used.
- **What is anchored.** A Knowledge concept that sits directly under the Knowledge root. A concept with a parent of its own keeps it, and a placement the person made is never touched. Each concept is considered once.
- **Exact names first.** A concept named like a field or subfield ("Computer science", "Artificial intelligence") becomes that frame node: it keeps its own node, name and parts, and gains the anchor.
- **Then extract → candidates → choose.** Code ranks the subfields by the words they share with the concept's name, definition and examples (rarer words count more; a word in the concept's name or the subfield's own name counts double) and keeps the top 10. One `map` call (purpose `anchor`) per refresh picks a subfield for each concept from its own list, or none. None is a normal answer: the concept stays where it was.
- **Placement.** An anchored concept goes under its subfield, and the subfield under its field, under Knowledge. Frame nodes are made only when something is placed under them, named as OpenAlex names them, and the person can rename or move them like any node (the anchor stays). Existing nodes are reused: a field or subfield node is found by its anchor, else by name.
- **Nothing else changes.** Frame nodes are concepts like any other: an untouched field is asked as an area card, and a middle answer opens parts the model writes, as now (decision 80). Splits below a concept are unchanged.
