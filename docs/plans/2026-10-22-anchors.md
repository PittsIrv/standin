# Anchors: the OpenAlex Frame. Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Top-level Knowledge concepts are placed under their OpenAlex subfield and field.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-22-anchors.md` (decisions 79–80).

## Global constraints

- The frame ships as data (CC0); no lookup leaves the machine.
- A placement the person made is never moved; a deleted frame node is never re-made; each concept is considered once.
- Commits: Mingxi is the author; every commit ends with the Co-Authored-By trailer.

## Review focus

- A concept the model answers "none" for, or anchors to something not on its list, stays where it was and isn't asked again.
- A concept named like a subfield whose name two fields share (e.g. "Genetics") is not matched by name.
- A field node the person renamed is still found by its anchor.
